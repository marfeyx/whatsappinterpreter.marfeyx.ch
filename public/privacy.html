Privacy Policy

Collected Data

The app reads WhatsApp export ZIP files locally in your browser. Chat messages, participants, timestamps, and media previews are processed on your device to render the viewer and statistics.

The exported ZIP, parsed chat contents, and generated preview object URLs are not intentionally uploaded to this site by the frontend.

Storage and Uploads

WhatsApp ZIP files are parsed locally. This version does not store WhatsApp exports or uploaded media in server storage. If storage is added later, this policy should be updated before launch to describe what is stored, why it is stored, and how deletion works.

Purpose of Processing

  • Render WhatsApp exports in a readable interface on the user's device.
  • Calculate local chat statistics and filtering results.

Retention

Locally parsed chat data remains in browser memory during the active session and is cleared when the page is refreshed, closed, or a new file replaces it.

User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data. For requests, contact the site operator.
