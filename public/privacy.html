Collected Data

The app reads WhatsApp export ZIP files locally in your browser. Chat messages, participants, timestamps, and media previews are processed on your device to render the viewer and statistics.

The exported ZIP, parsed chat contents, and generated preview object URLs are not intentionally uploaded to this site by the frontend.

Login and Account Data

password hash, session metadata, timestamps, and optional user metadata such as a display username derived from your email address.

Service role keys, database passwords, admin tokens, private API keys, and direct database connection strings must stay server-side and are not included in frontend code.

Storage and Uploads

WhatsApp ZIP files are parsed locally. This version does not store WhatsApp exports or uploaded media in stored, why it is stored, and how deletion works.

Purpose of Processing

  • Authenticate users before they open the local chat viewer.
  • Render WhatsApp exports in a readable interface on the user's device.
  • Calculate local chat statistics and filtering results.
  • Maintain account sessions and support account deletion.

Retention

Locally parsed chat data remains in browser memory during the active session and is cleared when the page is account or an administrator removes it according to the project's operational needs.

User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data. You can send an account deletion request from the app footer. For other requests, contact the site operator.
